## A little about me

**I learn best when there’s something to build.** AI, web development, and cybersecurity are where I spend a lot of my time. I like taking unfamiliar problems apart, trying ideas, and staying with them until they work.

I follow a problem wherever it takes me. Picking up a new tool or reading through something I don’t understand yet is part of the process.
